    Tools¶

    Supporting tooling for observability, reporting, developer experience and day-to-day development.

    • Metabase

      Business intelligence tool used to build dashboards and reports directly against operational data sources.

    • Grafana

      Open-source observability platform used to build the dashboards consulted during incident response.

    • Prometheus & Thanos

      Monitoring and alerting toolkit, extended with Thanos for high availability and long-term metric storage.

    • Backstage

      Open platform for building developer portals and maintaining a catalogue of services and their ownership.

    • IDEs

      Visual Studio Code, IntelliJ IDEA, PyCharm, PhpStorm and Visual Studio, selected according to the language and ecosystem of the project.
