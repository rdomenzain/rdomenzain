## Profile

Software engineer specialized in backend development, with professional
experience in the insurance, banking and intelligent transportation sectors.
The work has centred on designing and operating distributed systems that must
remain correct and available under sustained load, and on the platform tooling
that makes those systems maintainable by the teams that own them.

This site documents the technologies and platforms used in that work.
