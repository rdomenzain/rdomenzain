# Tools

The supporting cast: dashboards, metrics, developer portals and editors.

<div class="stack" markdown>

-   :simple-metabase: **Metabase**

    The easiest way to generate dashboards and reports straight from your data.

-   :simple-grafana: **Grafana**

    The open observability platform — where I put the graphs people actually
    look at during an incident.

-   :simple-prometheus: **Prometheus & Thanos**

    The open-source monitoring and alerting toolkit, plus a highly available
    Prometheus setup with long-term storage.

-   :simple-backstage: **Backstage**

    An open platform for building developer portals and taming service sprawl.

-   :material-application-braces-outline: **IDEs**

    Visual Studio Code, IntelliJ IDEA, PyCharm, PhpStorm, Visual Studio, and
    whatever else the project demands.

</div>
