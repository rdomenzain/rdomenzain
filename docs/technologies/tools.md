# Tools

Supporting tooling for observability, reporting, developer experience and
day-to-day development.

<div class="stack" markdown>

-   :simple-metabase: **Metabase**

    Business intelligence tool used to build dashboards and reports directly
    against operational data sources.

-   :simple-grafana: **Grafana**

    Open-source observability platform used to build the dashboards consulted
    during incident response.

-   :simple-prometheus: **Prometheus & Thanos**

    Monitoring and alerting toolkit, extended with Thanos for high availability
    and long-term metric storage.

-   :simple-backstage: **Backstage**

    Open platform for building developer portals and maintaining a catalogue of
    services and their ownership.

-   :material-application-braces-outline: **IDEs**

    Visual Studio Code, IntelliJ IDEA, PyCharm, PhpStorm and Visual Studio,
    selected according to the language and ecosystem of the project.

</div>
